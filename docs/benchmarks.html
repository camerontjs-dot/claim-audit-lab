Benchmarks & limits

Two different things get measured on this page, and conflating them is the failure mode this project most wants to avoid. Engineering integrity is verified and green: the tests pass, it installs, and deterministic bundle/report paths reproduce byte for byte when their run metadata is pinned. Real-inference regression uses a canonical decision receipt rather than claiming raw floating-point telemetry is cross-host identical. Measurement accuracy is not established. Whether CAL reaches the verdict a human reviewer would is the open question, and the numbers below say so.

Reproducibility

Determinism is the property CAL is willing to spend performance on. The inference stack is CPU-only by design so results do not move with accelerator availability.

Byte-identical outputs

With run id and timestamp pinned, C-B bundle audits reproduce byte for byte. Verified by golden tests in the suite above.

SHA-pinned rules

Thresholds live in cal-rules-v1.13.0.yaml. The loader pins its SHA-256 and fails loudly if a materialized config diverges.

Sealed research outputs

Probe directories carry checksum manifests. 39 of 40 re-verify. The fortieth is left failing on purpose: it was re-run in place on 2026-08-20, overwriting outputs the manifest still describes. Re-sealing would have hidden a real loss, so the manifest stands and the incident is DEV-005.

Measurement accuracy: not established

This is the honest part. CAL's engineering is sound; its agreement with human judgement is weak and well characterised. The abstention figures below are measured on scaled-30, a synthetic GxP/QMS corpus, and are DEV-grade. They do not describe the simple-logic gold lanes, where CAL returns supported cleanly: those are structurally easier by construction, and their passing is not evidence the abstention problem is solved.

26.5% abstention, PILOT-001 (human gold) 26 of 98 · the real FDA-derived corpus · stable across three runs
64.3% abstention, scaled-30 (synthetic GxP) 135 of 210 · single-document bundles by construction

The abstention rate is a property of the corpus, not a constant. The same pipeline abstains on 26.5% of human-gold claims and 64.3% of synthetic ones. And the reasons invert. On the synthetic corpus abstentions are overwhelmingly read_silent; on human gold the largest class is refutation_stood_down, where CAL measured a refutation and the rules layer deliberately declined to act on it.

The conflicting_signal row is the cautionary one. A sealed analysis of the synthetic corpus concluded that this reason code was dead weight, because no claim in 210 carried both support and refutation. Running the same classification over human gold fires it twice. The code was not dead; the corpus could not exercise it: its bundles are single-document, so they contain no off-topic passages by construction.

Nothing here is an accuracy claim. These are distributions of CAL's own recorded reasons, not agreement with human labels.

Reproduce either column with the shipped read-only command: it re-reads recorded traces, runs no model, and re-decides nothing: 

claim-audit explain path/to/traces --out-dir ./explanations

Failure taxonomy

Where a verdict goes wrong, it goes wrong in one of these places.

ModeLayerReported asDanger
Floor miss, support never clears retrieval 1 · Retrieve not_checkable / no_evidence high indistinguishable from "the document does not say that", when it may say it verbatim
Entailment miss, passage read, no position taken 2 · Entail not_checkable / no_entail_signal moderate at least records that evidence was read; currently mislabelled, should be unsupported
Comparison not performed, threshold rule not applied to a value 2 · Entail neutral at high confidence moderate the model is not distracted; it does not take the reasoning step
False contradiction, adverse verdict on a compound claim 3 · Rules contradicted high asserts the opposite of the evidence; open defect D8
Citation does not justify verdict, one passage recorded for a two-passage claim 3 · Rules correct verdict, wrong receipt high invisible to every agreement metric; in a regulated context the citation is the product

Are the abstentions right?

An abstention rate means nothing on its own. The question is whether CAL abstained on claims a human could actually settle. Crossing the 26 PILOT-001 abstentions against human gold, and separating out claims whose evidence was deliberately starved (where abstaining is the correct answer):

18 / 26 correctly abstained evidence starved by design, or gold also said not_checkable
8 / 26 questionable evidence was available and the human reached a verdict

Of the eight, three are the same mechanism: a refutation measured between 0.24 and 0.998 that P1_eligibility suppressed because the source's trust level does not permit it to solo-drive an adverse verdict. On two of those three, the human independently agreed the claim was unsupported or contradicted. CAL measured the right thing and discarded it.

That looks like an argument for a weak or hedged verdict. It is not, and the data says so plainly. Sweeping a weakly_supported verdict over every threshold is net-negative at all of them: at its best it recovers 2 claims while wrongly promoting 3. And a weak adverse verdict cannot be separated by score at all: the suppressed refutations that should be promoted score 0.9979 and 0.9948, while one that must not be sits between them at 0.9939. The populations interleave, so no threshold divides them.

●

The fix is reporting, not a new verdict. CAL already records why it stood down. Surfacing that ("a refutation was measured at 0.99 but the source is not eligible to carry an adverse verdict alone; read the passage") lets a reviewer catch the two CAL was right about, without CAL asserting the one false adverse verdict that any score-based promotion would also buy.

That output is what claim-audit explain emits today.

Open defect register

Named defects are tracked here rather than in an internal tracker because a claim-auditing tool that hid its own unsupported claims would be self-refuting. Open on this surface: numeric operators (D1, D4, D12), D6 (held), D7 (must not land without D6), and D14, a false adverse verdict found 2026-08-20 by running one realistic paragraph through the built wheel, measured across identifier shapes, and fixed in cal-rules-v1.13.0. And D15, a replay harness that reported a gate as a no-op when it could not fire the gate at all, and D16, found by the official release run: A7 withholding on an out-of-scope passage while an in-scope one refutes the claim. D10 and D11 were added 2026-08-19 by the construction-gold probe, which promoted D9 from a presentation defect to a verdict-changing one and then landed D9 and D10 together in cal-rules-v1.9.0, and D11 in cal-rules-v1.10.0. A7 (scope mismatch as a question) landed in cal-rules-v1.12.0, and v1.13.0 taught it to see identifiers containing a numeral (D14).

Run end to end it moves 2 of 98, both supported → not_checkable via A5_conflicting_evidence, and human gold says supported for both. Exact agreement on PILOT-001 is 62/98, not the 64/98 inherited from v1.7.0. The replay could not have seen it: A5 reads best_entail and best_contradict, fields that did not exist when the replayed baseline was recorded, so its precondition was unsatisfiable by construction. The evidence is identical either way: same passages, same labels, 0 of 98 claims differ in entailed-set size. See D15.

D11 followed in v1.10.0, taking the corpus to 22/33 and the adverse verdict to precision 6/6. No false adverse verdict remains on it. A no-op on the goldens, and on PILOT-001 as far as the end-to-end run can tell: the two movers above are A5's, not D11's. The obvious fix there was lexical, and it worked; it was rejected and rebuilt structurally because overlap may flag, never decide is a standing invariant, adopted after the v0.2 lexical matcher was falsified and the same error was re-imported once already.

v1.11.0 taught A6 to read the declared source_boundary, moving the three exhaustive absences and the named-gap case. Re-run on the shipped cal-rules-v1.13.0, the corpus stands at 26/33, adverse verdict precision 7/7 and recall 7/7, variant-group partition agreement 7/9.

All seven remaining misses are false abstentions. Every one is gold supported, CAL not_checkable; no false adverse verdict is left in the corpus at this rules version. Four of the 26 agreements are reached with zero passages entailed (right answer, no reasoning) and are named rather than banked. Sealed at outputs/2026-08-20-construction-gold-v1.12.0/.

Read that as a statement about the corpus, not the engine. D14 is a false adverse verdict on this rules version, found outside the corpus. Every A7 passage here is short, cleanly parsed, and free of alphanumeric identifiers, so the corpus never exercised the gate on the shapes that carry scope in a real regulated document. The absence of a failure in a corpus is evidence about the corpus first.

What would change these numbers

  • Relabelling abstentions. 128 of 135 abstentions have already had their evidence admitted and read. Reporting them as unsupported rather than not_checkable is a vocabulary fix, not a model fix.
  • D8 (narrow) landed. The live PILOT-001 wording of rsh-475fe956a5fb-c002 is no longer a contradicted under A3. Broader compound-negation forms remain open. D9 landed 2026-08-19, which also makes a cited passage match the evidence that produced the verdict.
  • A multi-source corpus. Built 2026-08-19. Single-document bundles had concealed two independent defects because every candidate passage is on-topic by construction. Adding out-of-scope passages surfaced a third (D9) within one run, but only once the distractors were built to clear the retrieval floor. Topically unrelated ones scored 0.13–0.30 against a 0.40 floor and never reached the component under test.
  • A gate on the current rule surface. The 2026-07-24 exploratory blind gate cleared on cal-rules-v1.7.0 (Gwet AC2 0.7901, weighted κ 0.6876, exact agreement 27/50). The two agreement coefficients are computed over 22 of the 50 items: not_checkable is off-scale, so CAL's most common outcome drops out of any complete-case statistic; 27/50 is the only headline figure over the whole set. The tree has since moved to v1.13.0 and six defects were found after it, so that clearance does not transfer unexamined, and the v1.9.0 landing costs 2 of the 98 PILOT-001 agreements when the pipeline is run end to end, a cost that a rules-only replay reported as zero because the gate's input fields postdate the baseline it replays (D15).
●

Engineering complete (mechanisms verified) ≠ formally validated. CAL's pipeline, determinism, and provenance behave as specified and are tested. Its agreement with expert human judgement is measured, weak, and unresolved. No claim of GxP validation, regulatory fitness, or production accuracy is made anywhere in this repository.
