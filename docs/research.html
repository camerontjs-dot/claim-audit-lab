Research

CAL is developed as an instrument, so its own behaviour is measured the same way anything else is. Each brief below is a write-up of a probe that ran against a sealed output directory. Where a brief and a sealed FINDINGS.md disagree, the sealed artifact wins.

●

Every result published here is DEV-grade. None of it is validation evidence, gate clearance, or an accuracy claim, and populations are small and often deliberately selected. Each brief states its own n and its own boundary, and those statements are the operative ones.

How these are produced

Sealed outputs

Each probe writes a dated directory with its script, raw results, per-claim traces, and a SHA256SUMS manifest. Raw result files are left exactly as the run produced them; corrections live in amendments beside them, never as edits.

Preregistered reads

Probes commit to their decision rule before the numbers exist, and carry identity controls: an inert restructure of the harness must reproduce the baseline exactly before any arm is read as a one-variable change.

Falsification counts

Hypotheses that die are recorded with the same weight as ones that survive. Several briefs exist because a probe falsified its own premise and found a defect while doing it.

Brief 02 2026-08-19 · DEV construction · n=33 · sealed

A gold that derives its own answers, and the coupled defect it found

The only human-labelled reference CAL has is measurably unreliable on absence claims: the coded verdict tracked how much material was in the bundle rather than any stated rule (Fisher exact p = 0.0008). Rather than argue with the coder, the reference was rebuilt so the answer is a function of how each case was constructed, regenerable, and impossible to disagree with.

CAL scored 14/33. The corpus is concentrated on suspected failure shapes, so that is a probe result and not an accuracy figure. What it settled is more useful than the score.

FindingnReading
Constructed contradictions the entailer caught at 0.975–0.996 7 / 7 The probabilistic stage did its job.
… that A4_negation_consistency then stood down 7 / 7 The deterministic stage discarded every one.
contradicted verdicts CAL did emit, all on silent sources 3 Precision 0/3, recall 0/7. It fires only where it should not.

The one that changed a plan: adding a single passage stating the same obligation for a different site flipped a correct supported to an abstention. max_entailment takes the highest score regardless of its label, so an out-of-scope passage scoring 0.016 higher overrode a correct 0.948 entailment, and A4 then suppressed the result. A4's over-suppression was masking the aggregation defect, so the two had to move together: replaying the real trace with the new gate blinded shows the A4 fix on its own producing a false contradicted on a correctly-sourced claim.

Both landed as cal-rules-v1.9.0, and the third defect (an adverse verdict on absence claims whose source is silent) as cal-rules-v1.10.0. v1.11.0 then taught A6 to read the declared source_boundary. Exact agreement 14→20→22→26 of 33; adverse-verdict precision 0/3→6/9→6/6→7/7 at recall 0/7→6/7→7/7. Twelve verdicts moved, every one wrong→right, and no false adverse verdict remains on the corpus. All seven misses that are left are gold supported read as not_checkable. All 30 frozen goldens hold. On human gold the picture is worse than first published: run end to end, v1.9.0's A5 gate moves 2 of 98 PILOT-001 verdicts from supported to not_checkable, against gold of supported. 64/98 becomes 62/98. The rules-only replay reported zero because A5's input fields postdate the baseline it replays (D15).

Baseline: outputs/2026-08-19-construction-gold/, measured on cal-rules-v1.8.0 · current result re-run on the shipped cal-rules-v1.13.0 and sealed at outputs/2026-08-20-construction-gold-v1.12.0/ · 4 of the 26 agreements are reached with zero passages entailed, which the audit counts and prints rather than banking.

Brief 01 2026-08-02 · DEV probe · n=12 · sealed

Why context allocation and semantic entailment fail on disjoint populations

Changing only the retrieval passage unit (sections to clauses) rescued 2 of 2 retrieval-floor failures and 0 of 10 entailment failures. A supporting clause that scores 0.392 inside its section scores 0.924 as its own unit: the same text, the same claim, the same encoder. The two layers fail on populations that do not overlap, so neither fix shrinks the other's problem.

PopulationnRescued by finer unitsReading
Retrieval-floor misses no_evidence 2 2 / 2 A unit problem. Solved outright.
Entailment misses no_entail_signal 10 0 / 10 Untouched. Mean neutral confidence rose to 0.9976.

What the probes have settled

Findings below are stated at the confidence the receipts support. They are engineering findings about this instrument on these corpora, not general claims about retrieval or NLI.

FindingStatusWhere
Retrieval and entailment fail on disjoint populations held Brief 01
Section-level chunking dilutes clause-level support below the floor held Brief 01
Adding section headers to fine units supplies useful context falsified Brief 01 §3.1, no material change
Section distractors are what confuse the entailer falsified Brief 01 §3.2, isolation raised neutral confidence
The v0.2 lexical matcher is a usable measurement instrument falsified 2026-06-18 blind calibration, 4/98 exact, κ ≈ −0.006

The falsified rows are the point, not an apology. The v0.2 result is why v1 exists, and two of the three hypotheses Brief 01 set out to test died in the process of measuring them.
