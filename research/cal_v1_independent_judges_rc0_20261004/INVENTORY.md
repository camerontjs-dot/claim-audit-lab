## Axis 2: cross-cutting assessments

Each relevant assessment must independently inspect the original claim/evidence, retain a local typed conclusion and basis, and declare applicability. None is automatically support/refutation of the whole claim.

- Claim interpretation and semantic coverage: roles, reference/coreference, ambiguous attachments, original-to-view conservation.
- Negation/polarity and scope: not higher is not lower; no/every/some; local negation versus a quoted/reporting assertion.
- Quantifier/cardinality/population/geography: five versus all provinces, almost all versus all groups, eight versus two instances.
- Measure/property, values, units, denominator and uncertainty: rate versus count, COPD versus asthma, percent versus percentage points, absolute versus relative change.
- Temporal identity/order/interval/currency: observation time versus publication time, overlapping periods, source versions/supersession.
- Conditions/exceptions and modality: conditional claims versus established events, required versus permitted, may versus does.
- Attribution and assertion ownership: source reports that X versus the evidence independently establishes X.
- Evidence eligibility, semantic validity, authority/supplier and jurisdictional applicability: explicit policy/receipt, never source-class shortcuts.
- Aperture/completeness and evidence sufficiency: no deciding relation is not contradiction; retrieval depth is not completeness.
- Provenance/integrity and input bindings: mechanical upstream commitments plus CAL claim-relative checks, no source legitimacy from hashes alone.

Status: all require explicit per-process implementation/qualification entries. Some bounded behavior exists in historical or current instruments; this list does not declare ten new fully implemented judges.

## Axis 3: measurement methods

Catalogue by exact implementation/model/prompt/config, input limits and tested domain, independently of the family list:

- Existing deterministic strict-comparison measurement and separate source completion/warrant.
- Existing deterministic event-order measurement and separate source completion/warrant.
- Structured claim/source parsing and local proposition proposals, with conservation checks.
- Numeric/unit/interval computations, with independently checked operands and dimensions.
- Learned entailment/NLI as a peer measurement, not sovereign relevance gate or automatic verdict.
- Model-assisted semantic judges with structured basis, input spans and retained raw outputs; no guessed model or calibration identity.
- Attribution/scope/eligibility/application-specific judges; qualification distinct from regex cue detection.
- Evidence aggregation/composition tests as synthesis machinery, not additional independent source evidence.

Same model or parser reused under several labels shares a dependence record. Separate prompts are not proof of independent errors. Relevance is not a retrieval similarity score. Raw probabilities from unlike models are not comparable confidence weights by default.

## Axis 5: losses and how to test them

| Lossy operation | Required local record | Discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Source extraction/OCR/normalization | raw representation if available, transform identity, offsets, unknown extraction state | meaning-changing punctuation/negation and raw-to-text disagreements; do not pretend unavailable bytes were read |
| Chunking/retrieval/admission/truncation | full available authorized world, consumed spans, omitted/truncated contexts | distant qualifier/counterevidence retained and missing context cannot become neutral support |
| Target authoring/family assignment | lane-local target, original claim binding, interpretation uncertainty | #193 four modifier collisions; unknown form must not globally starve other lanes |
| Decomposition | exact root, local children/operator, conservation result | root/direct disagreement and missing qualifier/connective/child |
| Embedding/NLI/semantic compression | model/config, actual tokens/spans, raw outputs, local hypothesis | lexical similarity without entailment; contradiction or qualifier despite high score |
| Numeric normalization | original value/unit/denominator/time and conversions | equal strings with different dimensions; percent vs points |
| Score or verdict aggregation | original local conclusions, eligibility, weights, conflicts, exclusions, dependence groups | irrelevant dilution, self-weighting, cloned votes, minority material refutation |
| Contract export/report summarization | immutable full audit record and public reconstruction requirements | disagreement/unknown/loss cannot disappear in report or downstream policy |
