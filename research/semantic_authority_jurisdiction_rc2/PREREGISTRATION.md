# Semantic Authority / Jurisdiction RC2 — Preregistration

## Classification

Research Infrastructure / epistemic-authority architecture experiment.

Parent authority is frozen CAL PR #41. This experiment does not alter production CAL, Contract C, thresholds, or the pinned NLI model.

## Question

Can CAL represent semantic decision authority as a typed jurisdiction relation:

`measurement -> applicability -> authority domain -> validity -> aggregation/decision`

such that:

1. a mechanism may only decide inside its declared semantic domain;
2. numeric/relational authority can validate numeric relations without inheriting generic contradiction authority;
3. source-boundary authority can validate completeness/absence relations without becoming evidence-support authority;
4. scope mismatches fail closed;
5. authority-domain cross-use is rejected;
6. downstream action authority remains conceptually separable from epistemic authority even if both use actor/operation/target/domain/currentness-style bindings.

## Hypotheses

H1 — Domain firewall:
A receipt from one semantic authority domain cannot decide a different domain.

H2 — Numeric jurisdiction:
A typed numeric relation operator can establish a bounded relation only when property, entity/scope, units, and relation geometry are compatible.

H3 — Composition boundary:
Numeric authority may establish a condition (for example threshold exceeded) without acquiring authority over a downstream composed conclusion such as Quality Hold.

H4 — Scope:
A numerically matching passage with the wrong scope is inapplicable and cannot decide.

H5 — Source boundary:
Absence/completeness authority is decidable only under an exhaustive aperture or an exact named-gap receipt. Bounded aperture remains unknown.

H6 — Cross-cutting shape:
Epistemic and action authority can share a structural jurisdiction pattern without sharing authority domains or semantic evaluators.

Falsify H6 if safe operation requires collapsing semantic validity and execution permission into one authority state.
