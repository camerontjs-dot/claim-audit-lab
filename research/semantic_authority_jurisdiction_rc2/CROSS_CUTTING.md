# Cross-cutting authority structural comparison

This experiment distinguishes **shared structure** from **shared authority**.

Decision Engine PR #21 observed action/jurisdiction bindings over actor, typed operation, exact target/currentness, participant effect domain, and standing grant.

CAL RC2 tests epistemic semantic authority over typed semantic domain, operation, exact target, applicability/currentness, and validity status.

The candidate common shape is therefore:

```text
authority subject/mechanism
typed domain
typed operation
exact target
currentness
applicability
receipt/basis
outcome
```

The authority domains remain disjoint:

- epistemic semantic authority does not grant execution permission;
- execution authority does not establish semantic truth;
- source-access authority does not establish evidence relevance;
- evidence-admission authority does not establish support;
- CAL support does not grant citation authority.

A passing structural comparison supports a common **authority interface pattern**, not one universal authority evaluator.
