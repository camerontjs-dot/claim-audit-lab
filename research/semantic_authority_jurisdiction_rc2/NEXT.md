# NEXT — after Semantic Authority / Jurisdiction RC2

## NEW EXPERIMENT REQUIRED

The remaining numeric uncertainty is upstream of the typed validator.

Test whether an independent extraction layer can recover, from raw claim/evidence text:

- property identity;
- entity/scope identity;
- value;
- unit;
- relation geometry;
- threshold direction;
- temporal qualifier where applicable;

without seeing expected CAL verdicts.

Freeze extraction outputs before semantic-validation comparison.

Required mutations:

- same number, different property;
- same number, different scope;
- same quantity, convertible units;
- same surface number, different temporal role;
- point vs maximum/minimum;
- approximate vs exact;
- range vs midpoint;
- conjunction requiring composition.

Do not give the extractor semantic decision authority. Its authority should end at typed representation.

Separately, retain source-boundary authority as its own domain and do not merge it into numeric or NLI validity.

No production promotion until independent extraction and an independent consumer both succeed.
