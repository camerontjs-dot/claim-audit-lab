## Question

What proposal machinery can recover **explicit typed relation assertions** without laundering contribution, correlation, precedence, co-occurrence, ambiguous association, or experimental context into `CAUSES`?

This is not a causal-inference experiment.

The frozen kind inventory is:

- `CAUSES`
- `CONTRIBUTES_TO`
- `PREVENTS`
- `CORRELATES_WITH`
- `PRECEDES`
- `CO_OCCURS`

## Claim buckets

MUST_HANDLE:
- one direct canonical surface for every frozen kind;
- a second endpoint binding to expose endpoint erasure.

- passive cause;

- `occurs together with`.

FAIL_CLOSED:
- reporting wrapper;
- epistemic modality;
- ambiguous risk-factor language;
- prediction;
- conjunction/disjunction over effects;

- generic association;
- `explains`;
- logical sufficiency.

## Acceptance rule

A path is `QUALIFIABLE_FOR_GATE1B` only if:

- every MUST_HANDLE case is exact;
- no FAIL_CLOSED case is claimed;
- any claimed DIAGNOSTIC is exact;
- replay is deterministic;
- frozen metamorphic controls pass;
- production `src/` remains unchanged.

A pass qualifies measurement of explicit assertion kind only. It does not warrant the assertion and does not infer causality from data, experiments, order, statistics, or multiple passages.
