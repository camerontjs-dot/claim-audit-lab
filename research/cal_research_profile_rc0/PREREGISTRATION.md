# CAL Research Profile RC0 strict-comparison preregistration

## Decision

Can the already-supported strict-comparison research lineage be connected into a coherent research-only B→CAL→C vertical without recreating the stale-authority, scalar-shortcut, proposition-substitution, mixed-relation, or Contract-C-strengthening failures?

## Candidate boundary

Only `strict_comparison` can produce deciding proposition relations. Event ordering and permission-composition may be measured only in shadow and are not used by this runtime. Every unsupported semantic family is an explicit unresolved blocker. No released-CAL fallback is permitted.

A deciding relation requires, in order:

1. exact Contract B 1.2.0 validator PASS;
2. evidence admitted for the exact claim/passage;
3. RC7F-B1 bounded comparison measurement;
4. complete strict-comparison atom with exact source spans and all material fields established;
5. exact frozen RC8J `WARRANTED` for the same atom/evidence/claim payload;
6. atom-bound warrant receipt verified against the exact consumed payload;
7. exact claim text + typed proposition binding verified;
8. categorical relation derived from those exact consumed values;
9. scoreless fail-closed composition;
10. conservative Contract C 1.0.0 projection that cannot strengthen the internal result.

## Twelve prereveal seam/falsifier cases

1. stale warrant + changed atom;
2. valid atom receipt + changed typed proposition;
3. changed claim text under the same identifier;
4. comparison direction reversal;
5. entity/role substitution;
6. non-warranted atom attempting participation;
7. unsupported semantic family attempting participation;
8. one scoreless composition matrix covering support-only, refutation-only, irrelevant-only, unresolved-only, support+irrelevant, support+unresolved, support+refutation, and incomplete-required composition;
9. confidence/score perturbation invariance;
10. reader/agreement-count perturbation invariance;
11. Contract C may not strengthen internal abstention;
12. incomplete/unmeasured atom cannot be repaired by confidence or agreement metadata.

Any acceptance of a stale/foreign receipt, proposition substitution, unsafe support/refutation, scalar/count terminal influence, mixed-relation winner, or Contract-C strengthening is a hard falsifier.

## Freeze and smoke order

Implementation and the seam evaluator freeze first. The machine-readable profile manifest freezes second and identifies those exact bytes. Only after that profile freeze may the 6–8-case naturalistic cohort and human gold be created. A separate smoke execution manifest must bind the frozen profile manifest to the exact cohort/gold identities before execution. No runtime/evaluator repair is permitted after reveal.
