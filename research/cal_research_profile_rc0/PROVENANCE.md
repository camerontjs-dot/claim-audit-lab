# CAL Research Profile RC0 strict-comparison provenance

Status: Draft Research Infrastructure only. No production CAL, released rule, Contract B/C, Decision Engine, Contract D, Contract E, release, tag, or promotion surface is modified.

## New seam

RC0 adds one deliberately narrow engineering seam: an exact RC7F-B1 comparison measurement proposal must be completed into a strict-comparison atom using only fields unambiguously established in the admitted passage. If the proposal is absent, ambiguous, qualified outside the bounded affirmative grammar, or lacks a material quantitative field that RC0 would need to represent, the case stops `UNRESOLVED`. Confidence, score, reader count, agreement count, retrieval rank, and model count are not consumed by the authority or composition path.
