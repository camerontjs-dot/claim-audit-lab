Can a stricter independent source-completion layer preserve the current clean strict-comparison measurement jurisdiction while refusing sources whose material negation, epistemic modality, or attribution is not represented in the comparison atom?

## Frozen boundaries

The successor may change only research-side authority/source-completion machinery.

It may not:
- change the production strict measurement instrument;
- widen the comparison grammar;
- change relation algebra;
- change production `src/`;
- infer modifier semantics into a positive relation;
- rely on the measurement proposal as source authority.

## Required clean jurisdiction

The candidate must independently reconstruct and warrant:
- direct adjective comparison;
- direct comparative verb;
- direct lower-than comparison;
- a numeric-delta comparison supported by the production measurement.

## Required refusals

The candidate must refuse after a CLAIMED production measurement:
- `not higher than`;
- `no higher than`;
- `probably higher than`;
- `allegedly higher than`;
- `may be higher than`;
- `could be higher than`;
- report-attributed comparison.

## Warrant contract

A candidate may issue a warrant only when:
1. the production measurement receipt is internally valid;
2. it is the exact strict-comparison instrument identity;
3. it consumed exactly the named admitted passage;
4. an independent source parser reconstructs exactly one supported unmodified comparison;
5. reconstructed source fields equal the measurement fields.

The source parser must reject material modifier/scope cues before relation reconstruction.
