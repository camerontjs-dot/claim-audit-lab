                              Special Note

Each September, NASS has the opportunity to revise planted and harvested acreage
estimates for chickpeas, corn, cotton, dry edible peas, lentils, peanuts, rice,
sorghum, soybeans, and sugarbeets. Revisions are based on all available data,
including the latest certified acreage data from the Farm Service Agency (FSA). All
States in the estimating program for these crops were subject to review and
updating.
